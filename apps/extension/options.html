Planner for Canvas

This extension reads your Canvas assignments with your own login and sends them to your planner server. It only ever reads: nothing is written back to Canvas.

1. Pair with your planner server

On the server's settings page, generate a pairing code, then enter it here within 15 minutes. Status:

2. Your school's Canvas address

Canvas on school.instructure.com is found by itself: open it once while signed in. Beta and test copies (school.beta.instructure.com, school.test.instructure.com) are never synced, because they carry the same assignment ids as the real one. If your school uses its own address (for example canvas.school.edu), sign in there in another tab, then add it here and allow the permission prompt.

Status

    Not synced:

      "Forget everything" removes the pairing, your Canvas addresses, sync results and the extra permissions from this browser. The device stays listed on the planner's settings page until you remove it there.

      The "Plan my week" button

      The dashboard button opens the assistant chosen on the planner's settings page with a planning prompt. ChatGPT sends a prompt passed in its address (?q=) as soon as the page opens, before you can turn the planner connector on for that chat: enable the connector in ChatGPT first, or the first answer will not see your Canvas.
