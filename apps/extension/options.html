Planner for Canvas

This extension reads your Canvas assignments with your own login (read-only) and sends them to your planner server. Nothing is written back to Canvas.

1. Pair with your planner server

On the server's settings page, generate a pairing code, then enter it here within 15 minutes. Status:

2. Your school's Canvas address

Instances on *.instructure.com work out of the box. If your school uses its own address (for example canvas.school.edu), add it here and allow the permission prompt.
